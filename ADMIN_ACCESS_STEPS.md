✅ Account created! You'll be redirected to login page.

## 🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING:

**Problem**: "Invalid admin secret key"
- **Solution**: Make sure you typed exactly: `ALOWEDA_ADMIN_2024` (case-sensitive)
